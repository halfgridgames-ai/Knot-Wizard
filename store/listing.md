**App name:** Knot Wizard

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Relaxing rope puzzle: untangle the knots and reveal hidden pictures. Offline.

**Full description:**
A tiny wizard has tangled every rope in the kingdom, and only you can untie them.

Knot Wizard is a relaxing untangle puzzle and brain teaser. Drag the wooden pins until no two ropes cross. When the last knot clears, the wizard casts his spell, and sometimes the ropes snap into a hidden picture.

HIDDEN PICTURES
• 30 secret pictures hide inside the knots: a sailboat, a cat, a rocket, a magic gem and more
• Collect every one in your Spellbook

A NEW DAILY KNOT EVERY DAY
• A mystery picture puzzle every day, with a different twist for each weekday
• Keep your streak going for bonus hints, and get a gentle reminder when the next knot is ready

150 LEVELS, 5 WORLDS
• The Forest, the Castle, the Docks, the Clouds and the spinning Void
• Every world adds a new twist: locked iron pins, stretchy ropes, tied pins and a board that never stops turning
• Earn up to 3 stars per level by solving in as few moves as you can

MADE TO RELAX
• One finger is all you need, with undo and restart always there
• Stuck? The wizard shows you the next move
• A calm soundtrack with its own tune for every world
• Unlock rope styles with stars: licorice, iron chain, fairy lights and rainbow silk
• Play offline, anywhere, no Wi-Fi needed

If you enjoy logic puzzles, untangle games, knot puzzles or calm brain training, Knot Wizard is for you.

Knot Wizard contains optional ads and in-app purchases.
