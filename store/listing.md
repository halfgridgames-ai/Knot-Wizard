**App name:** Knot Wizard

**Short description (80 characters max):**

**Full description:**
A tiny wizard has tangled every rope in the kingdom, and only you can untie them.

Knot Wizard is a calm, satisfying puzzle game. Drag the wooden pins around the board until no two ropes cross. When the last knot clears, the ropes snap tight and the wizard does a little dance.

• 150 levels across 5 worlds: the Forest, the Castle, the Docks, the Clouds and the spinning Void
• Every world adds a twist: locked iron pins, stretchy ropes, tied pins and a board that never stops turning
• A calm soundtrack with its own tune for every world
• Earn up to 3 stars on every level by finishing in as few moves as you can
• A new Daily Knot every day, with a streak to keep
• Stuck? The wizard gives you hints
• Unlock rope styles as you collect stars: licorice, iron chain, fairy lights and rainbow silk
• One finger is all you need, and undo is always there
• Play offline, anywhere

Knot Wizard contains optional ads and in-app purchases.
