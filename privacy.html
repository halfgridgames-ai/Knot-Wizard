Knot Wizard privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Published by Halfgrid Games ("we").

Knot Wizard is a rope-untangling puzzle game. It has no accounts, no online play, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data. This page explains what the game stores and which third-party services it uses.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, hints, chosen rope style, daily streak and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them. We never receive this information.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android and iPhone apps show ads through Google AdMob: optional video ads you choose to watch for hints, and occasional ads between levels unless you buy Remove Ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and EU), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in your device settings. On iPhone, the game only uses your advertising ID if you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. We do not see or store your payment details. The store tells the game which items you have bought so it can unlock them, and so they can be restored if you reinstall. See the Google privacy policy and the Apple privacy policy.

Children

Knot Wizard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Skip optional video ads, or buy Remove Ads to stop ads between levels.
  • Reset or limit ad personalisation in your device's Google settings (Android), or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking (iPhone).
  • Uninstall the game to delete everything it stored on your device.
